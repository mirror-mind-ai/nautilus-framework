# Identity

## Name

**Nautilus Agentic Framework** is the name of the conceptual and pedagogical body of the [Nautilus Family](family.md), oriented to understanding and practicing the framework.

**Nautilus** names the family that coordinates the Framework, App, and Protocol.

## Version

```yaml
framework: Nautilus Agentic Framework
method_version: 0.1.0-experimental
grammar_status: experimental
```

This version is experimental because it does not yet intend to stabilize the complete ontology. It stabilizes a first surface of language to allow reading, experimentation, and common recognition among the Nautilus Family bodies.

`method_version` remains the compatibility field used by the Protocol. It does not retain the Framework's former name.

## Nominal family

The [Nautilus Family](family.md) has three bodies:

| Body | Name | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Framework | Nautilus Agentic Framework | Human, conceptual, and pedagogical language |
| App | Nautilus App | Practical application experience, delivered as Nautilus for Mirror |
| Protocol | Nautilus Agentic Protocol | Formal, portable, and verifiable declaration of the grammar |

The family exists so that the same grammar can be understood by humans, made practicable in an application experience, and declared formally.

## Recognition fields

The first vertical identity expects all bodies to recognize these minimal fields:

```yaml
name: Nautilus
method_version: 0.1.0-experimental
protocol_version: 0.1.0
schema_version: 0.1.0
grammar_status: experimental
compatibility: compatible
```

These fields do not make the bodies identical. They preserve recognizability.

## Naming decisions

[`Participant`](participant.md) is the canonical neutral term for whoever participates in the grammar. A Participant may have human or agentic nature.

`Nauta` is not a canonical category of the framework in this version.

Terms such as Crew, Crew member, or Navigator may inspire experiential language in the App, but do not replace the ontology. The nautical metaphor orients the framework's sensitivity. It should not colonize every concept.

## Boundary

This identity does not define execution, persistence, roles, authority, complete [Evidence](evidence.md), or operational [Integration](integration.md). These elements appear as horizon of the grammar, but enter only when a future slice can sustain them coherently across the three bodies.

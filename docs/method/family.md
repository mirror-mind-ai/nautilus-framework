# Nautilus Family

The Nautilus Family brings together the three bodies that let the same grammar exist as human framework, practical application experience, and formal declaration.

The family is not a collection of independent projects. It preserves coherence between what the framework means, what the app makes practicable, and what the protocol declares, without collapsing their distinct responsibilities.

## Bodies

| Body | Name | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Framework | Nautilus Agentic Framework | Conceptual and pedagogical body, oriented to understanding and practicing the framework |
| App | Nautilus App | Practical application experience for the framework, delivered as Nautilus for Mirror |
| Protocol | Nautilus Agentic Protocol | Formal, portable, and verifiable declaration of the grammar |

## Nautilus Agentic Framework

The Framework gives language, meaning, and human criteria to practice.

It explains what [Value Generation](value-generation.md), a Journey, a Mission, a Delivery, Evidence, a [Realization](realization.md), [Impact](impact.md), and Integration mean for whoever uses Nautilus.

Its function is to prevent the family from becoming merely technical. Before a form is declared or made practicable in the app, the framework must make its human meaning understandable and teachable.

## Nautilus App

The App is the practical application experience for the framework. It delivers Nautilus for Mirror, where the shared grammar becomes situated work for human and agentic Participants.

Its function is to make the framework usable without replacing its common ontology. The App owns its product and runtime implementation surface.

## Nautilus Agentic Protocol

The Protocol translates the framework language into declarative form.

It defines fields, versions, compatibility, and recognizable structures. Its function is to allow the grammar to be portable, validatable, and shareable across implementations.

## Coherence between bodies

Each body speaks in a different register, but all must recognize the same form.

If the Framework names a concept, the Protocol must be able to declare it when it becomes formal. If the Protocol requires a field, the Framework must justify its human meaning. If the App operates a behavior, it must remain legible within the common grammar without becoming the authority that defines it.

This coherence is how the family grows without losing form.

## Historical note

Until November 2026, the Nautilus Family named four bodies. Nautilus Mirror Extension and Nautilus Harness are now historical bodies; the Harness continues as Nautilus App.

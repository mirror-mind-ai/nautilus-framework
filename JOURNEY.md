# Nautilus Agentic Framework
**Status:** active
**Stage:** Framework consolidation after family formation

## Description

Nautilus Agentic Framework is the conceptual and pedagogical body of the Nautilus family. It gives human and agentic Participants a shared framework for Value Generation through Journeys, Missions, Deliveries, Evidence, Realization, Impact and Integration.

It owns the framework language, principles, cases and teachable practice, while the Nautilus App and Nautilus Agentic Protocol own their respective practical and formal declaration surfaces. Historical conversations, tasks and records remain attached to this Journey as provenance.

## Current focus

Recontextualize the existing method corpus as the Nautilus Agentic Framework, preserve its conceptual grammar, establish clear family boundaries, and evolve the framework through bilingual documentation, canonical cases and teachable practices.
